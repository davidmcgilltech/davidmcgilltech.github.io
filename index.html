David McGill, Owner

I build custom web applications and business software for small companies in the Oklahoma City metro. Restaurants, non-profits, insurance agencies, service contractors, and school athletic departments have all hired me to replace a subscription tool with something they own outright.

What I build

  • Custom web applications. Scheduling, job orders, quoting, inventory, and reporting systems built around how your business already works instead of forcing it into someone else's template.
  • Internal tools and integrations. Connecting the systems you already pay for so the data stops being retyped by hand between them.
  • Mobile apps. Native iOS applications for teams that work away from a desk.
  • Websites with an admin you control. Public sites where you add the content yourself, without a monthly platform fee for the privilege.
  • Website maintenance. Ongoing updates, dependency upgrades, and fixes for software that is already running.

How engagements work

  1. Fixed fee, quoted up front. You get the price before work starts, not an open hourly meter.
  2. Paid by milestone. Work is split into deliverables you can see and use. You pay as each one lands.
  3. You own everything at the end. The source code, the database, the hosting accounts, and the domain are yours. There is no license to renew and nothing to lose access to if we stop working together.
  4. A support window is included. Every project ships with a defined period of included support so you are not on your own the day after launch.

Renting software costs you every month and leaves you with nothing. Buying it once costs more on day one and leaves you with an asset. That is the whole pitch.
