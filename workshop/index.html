AI workflows for small business owners

You have probably tried ChatGPT, gotten a mediocre answer, and gone back to doing the work by hand. Meanwhile the follow-up emails, quotes, customer replies, and weekly admin still eat hours you do not have. This is a hands-on, three-hour session where you build AI workflows for those exact tasks, using your own work, and leave with things you can run the next morning.

It is not a lecture and it is not a tour of tools. You do not need to be technical. You need a laptop and a short list of the jobs you are tired of doing.

What you leave with

  • Working workflows built on your own tasks. Follow-up after a quote, replies to common customer questions, proposals, social posts, meeting notes turned into action items. You pick the ones that cost you the most time, and we build them in the room.
  • Reusable templates. The prompts you build are saved in a form you can hand to an employee, so the result does not depend on you remembering how you phrased it.
  • A plain rule for what never goes into an AI tool. Customer records, financials, anything under a confidentiality agreement. You will know where the line is and why.
  • A way to check the output. These tools are confidently wrong often enough to embarrass you. You will learn the quick checks that catch it before a customer does.
  • A one-page action plan. What to put into use this week, what to try next, and what is not worth automating at all.

How the session runs

  1. Before the session, you send me your list. Two or three recurring tasks that eat your week. I shape the examples around what the group actually does.
  2. Ground rules. What these tools are good at, where they fail, and what stays out of them. Short, because the rest of the time is for building.
  3. We build one workflow together. Start to finish, on a real example, so you see every step and every correction.
  4. You build your own. Guided work time on your tasks. I work with each person in turn until theirs is working.
  5. You write your action plan. Afterward I email the templates and a written summary to everyone who attended.

Who it is for

Owners and managers of small service businesses: contractors, agencies, offices, restaurants, non-profits. People who write the same kinds of emails and documents every week and suspect a good share of it could be faster. If you already build your own automations or write code, this session will be too basic for you, and I would rather tell you that now than take your money.

Who teaches it

I am David McGill. I build custom software for small businesses in the Oklahoma City metro, and much of my career has been in software quality: deciding what should be automated, and proving whether the automation can be trusted.
